## Admin shell (`/admin`, `app/admin/layout.tsx`, `components/admin/admin-sidebar.tsx`)
- Outside the app shell: its own sidebar (logo + Admin tag, the admin's pages, Back to IDEEZA) from `md`, a bar over the page below it, and its own `<main>`. `/admin` opens AI credit pricing.
- Only `<main>` scrolls; it is `relative`, and so is each table's scroll box, so the tables' screen-reader text (`sr-only`, absolutely placed) stays inside them. Without it that text stretched the window to the tables' height (a second scrollbar) and, at phone width, `<main>` sideways.
- **No Sign up or Sign in** (added 2026-11-11, owner: "if browse /admin then if not logged in make a demo login. so this prototype reviewer no need to get signup/sign hasel for now"): the gates leave `/admin` alone (`isAdminPath`, in `AUTH_PREPAINT` and `AuthGate`, as the mobile prototype's `/m` is), and `AdminDemoSignIn` (`components/admin/admin-demo-sign-in.tsx`, around the layout's `<main>`) signs a visitor who arrives signed out in as **Demo Admin** (`demo.admin@ideeza.test`; `signInDemoAdmin`, made verified the first time through the provider sign-in, with a password nobody knows), shows *Signing you in as the demo admin…* and reloads, as every sign-in does (work-vault.ts brings the account's own work back). One sign-in per page load, however often React runs the effect. Someone already signed in stays themselves; signing out in another tab later leaves the page as it is. The sidebar's foot says *Signed in as …*, the name a publish records. Back to IDEEZA opens Home as Demo Admin. Storage blocked: *The demo couldn't sign you in*. The demo has no roles, so any signed-in account can open the admin too.
- **The prices are the browser's, not an account's:** `ideeza:admin:credit-pricing` is in work-vault's `BROWSER_KEYS`, so signing out never parks it and the demo admin's sign-in never drops it as a loose key; every account here sees the same prices, as every admin of the real platform would.
- `/admin` goes on to `/admin/ai-credits` as an HTTP redirect (`next.config.ts`), before anything renders: the page's own `redirect()` (`app/admin/page.tsx`, kept for `/admin/`) threw in development (React's render timing: *'AdminHome' cannot have a negative time stamp*), a red issue on a reviewer's first page.

## AI credit pricing (`/admin/ai-credits`, `/admin/credit-packs`, `lib/admin/`, `components/admin/credit-pricing/`)
- **The formula** (`lib/admin/credit-pricing.ts`, pure, `tests/projects/admin-credit-pricing.test.mjs`):
  - lowest effective credit price = credit value × (1 − maximum credit discount) ÷ (1 + maximum pack bonus)
  - credits = round up(cost × (1 + markup) ÷ the credit price the markup is calculated at), in steps of 1, 5 or 10, never less than one step
  - The % is a markup on cost (cost × 1.5 at 50%), not a margin on price. Calculated at the lowest effective credit price by default, so the markup holds for every buyer; at the list price, a row can earn less than its markup or lose money and says so.
- **What a credit is worth:** the dollar price is typed. USDT follows it one for one, and IDZ follows the IDZ price ($0.025065 seeded from `lib/wallet/idz-price.ts`), each with a switch to set it by hand. Each shows *Current X → Y*.
- **Markup:** *Markup on our cost* for every service and level; *Calculate markup at* (Lowest effective credit price or List price); *Maximum credit discount* (35%, the app's `MAX_DISCOUNT`) and *Maximum pack bonus* (15%, the biggest `CREDIT_PACKS` bonus); rounding. Under them, *The lowest effective credit price is $X* and a worked example ($0.10 run).
- **AI services:** 17 services in 8 groups. Columns (renamed 2026-11-11): AI service · Our cost per run · Markup (empty uses the global one) · Current credits · Calculated credits · Maker pays (list price) · Revenue at lowest price · Profit at lowest price (dollars and % on cost) · Pricing status (On target · Below target · Loses money · No cost yet). A row with no cost keeps its current price. Headings wrap to two lines so the nine columns fit a 1440 px screen; narrower, the table scrolls in its card.
- **Custom credit** (added 2026-11-11, from the live admin's Base Settings › Custom Credit): an *Enabled* switch in the header, *Minimum purchase limit* and *Maximum purchase limit* in credits, each with its dollar cost at list price (*315 credits cost $1.00 at list price.*) and *Current X → Y*. Seeded as the live admin has it: on, 315, 0 (no limit). Two checks the live admin lacks: the minimum is 1 or more, and the maximum is 0 or at least the minimum (*Enter 0 for no limit, or 315 or more*). Off shows *makers can buy packs only*; the limits are kept. The live admin's check that a shown pack's base credits reach the minimum is on the Credit packs tab (below). Changes wait for Publish like a price.
- **Product build quality:** Free · Low · Medium · High · Max, the same columns, headed *Quality level* and *Our cost per build*. Free is always 0 credits and shows its cost as ours. Above it, a section of its own, *Availability and daily limits* (added 2026-11-11, owner's ask; first under the prices in the same card, then moved above them the same day: "move Availability and daily limits section top of Product build quality", so the page reads AI services → Availability and daily limits → Product build quality): per level an *Offered to makers* switch and a *Daily limit per maker* (0 = no limit), with *Current On · No limit → …*. A level that's off keeps its price for when it's back on and reads *Off: makers can't pick it* in the price table; every level off is an error (*Keep at least one level on, or makers can't start a build.*). **Backend:** `AIQualityCost` has no on/off field, and it counts `daily_limit` only while `credit_cost` is 0, so both need backend work before they can be real.
- **Two tabs** (added 2026-11-11), each a page of its own: *Prices* (`/admin/ai-credits`, everything above) and *Credit packs* (`/admin/credit-packs`). They are links under the page header, styled as the project page's tab strip (as the Earn tabs are), and the admin sidebar lists both pages, so the packs can't be missed. Both pages share the summary, Review and publish and the history, and count both.
- **Credit packs** (added 2026-11-11, owner's ask, from the live admin's AI Credit System › Credit Packs; `lib/admin/credit-packs.ts`, `components/admin/credit-pricing/credit-packs-tab.tsx`): the admin types only a pack's **name and price**; its credits are worked out:
  - base credits = round down(price ÷ the list price of a credit, to the rounding step); bonus = round down(base × the tier's %, to the step); total = base + bonus. Rounding is down, so a pack never gives more than its price buys.
  - *Bonus by pack price*: tiers (*$0.00, the first tier* · from $10 · $25 · $50; 0 · 5 · 10 · 15%), at most 6; a pack takes the last tier its price reaches. No tier above the *Maximum pack bonus* under Markup (*Enter 0 to 15: the maximum pack bonus is 15%*), so the lowest effective credit price, and every markup, still holds; a bigger pack never gets a smaller bonus. *Round pack credits down to steps of* 1 · 10 · 50 · 100 (10).
  - Table, cheapest first: Pack name (*New*) · Most popular (a star, one pack at most) · Pack price · Base credits · Bonus (+n, %) · Total credits (*Current n* when Publish changes it) · Price per credit (*n% off list*) · Shown to makers (switch) · ⋮ Edit / Delete (confirm). *Add new pack* (12 at most) opens a dialog, *Pack name* and *Pack price*, with the base, bonus and total shown as they type.
  - Checks: a name (32 characters, none twice); a price over $0 up to $10,000 in cents; at least 10 base credits, and a shown pack's base at least the Custom credit minimum (the live admin's PR-8937; *Fewer than 315 credits, the minimum purchase. Raise the price to $1.02 or more, or hide the pack.*), so a hidden one only carries a note; with Custom credit off, at least one pack shown (*Makers can't buy credits: show a pack, or turn Custom credit on.*). A wrong pack or tier stops Publish (*Fix the credit packs first.*).
  - Changes wait for Publish like a price: *Starter pack: Not sold → $5.00 for 1,570 credits*, a new price, a name, Shown ↔ Hidden, *Removed*, *Most popular pack: Pro → Lab*.
  - Not modelled: the live packs' validity in months and their own USDT and IDZ prices (the live form types them; here they'd follow the credit's). **Backend:** `/ai-credit-packages/` takes `credit_amount` and `bonus_credits` as typed numbers; working them out from the price is this page's job until the backend does it.
